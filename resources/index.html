💡 Eligibility Myth-Buster • New York State

Does Your Rent Include Heat? You Still Might Qualify for New York HEAP

Most renters assume government energy programs are only for homeowners paying big monthly utility bills—so thousands of New Yorkers leave money on the table every winter. Watch this 1-minute breakdown on how NY HEAP actually works for renters.

1-min explainer by @PantryPeeps ▶ Watch on YouTube

What Renters Often Don’t Realize:

  • Heat-Included Rentals Qualify: Whether you rent a standard apartment, public housing, a senior complex, or a HUD-capped building with heat built into your rent, New York State issues a direct baseline benefit.
  • Savings in the Bank Are Allowed: For regular seasonal HEAP, having money saved in your bank account does not disqualify you—eligibility is based on household size and income brackets.
  • Regular vs. Emergency Timing: Regular seasonal HEAP benefits open in the fall, while emergency shut-off assistance opens in January.
  • Earned Public Program: HEAP is funded by the taxes you pay into—not a handout—and takes just a few minutes to check online or by phone.
