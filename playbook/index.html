Pantry-stretching guides

Mayo when you’re out of eggs

Use 3–4 single-serve mayonnaise packets as a substitute for one egg in muffins, quick breads, brownies, boxed cake, and drop cookies. Avoid recipes that depend on whipped eggs, such as meringues or sponge cakes.

Build a shelf-stable meal

Start with a filling base, add a protein and vegetables, and use a sauce or seasoning to bring it together. Check the Recipes hub for meal ideas.
