Seasonal Giving Guide (Outdoor Pantries)

Most Little Free Pantries sit outdoors, fully exposed to the elements. This guide is specifically for those outdoor boxes—not climate-controlled indoor 24/7/365 pantries, which can safely hold all shelf-stable items year-round.

Check the weather in your area before leaving anything. Do not assume that items will go fast and beat an incoming storm. If freezing temps are happening in the next 24–48 hours, skipping the following items can really help the pantry stewards—less mess is less work and less wasted food.

❄️ Freezing Weather (Below 32°F)

Please skip the following items: Cold & Stop
Please consider donating these instead: Go & Stock
Glass Jars:

Pasta sauce, salsa, jelly, baby food, peanut butter.

Canned Goods & Liquids:

Soups, canned veggies, bottled water, juice boxes, broth.

Wet Squeeze Pouches:

Applesauce, fruit, or watery baby food pouches.

Why: Glass jars crack and shatter, cans burst, plastic bottles split, and wet pouch seams rupture when frozen—creating dangerous broken glass and spoiled, ruined food inside the box.
Dry Pantry Staples:

Pasta, rice, instant mashed potatoes, dry soup mixes, stuffing, dried beans.

Low-Moisture Pouch Foods:

Microwave rice pouches, pouch refried beans, seasoned grain pouches, tuna/chicken foil pouches.

Baby & Toddler:

Powdered infant formula, dry baby cereal, teething biscuits, puffs.

Breakfast:

Oatmeal packets, just-add-water pancake mix, grits, cereal, Pop-Tarts, powdered eggs.

Snacks & Desserts:

Crackers, cookies, chips, pretzels, nuts, trail mix, granola bars, dehydrated fruits/vegetables, popcorn, shelf-stable baked goods, Jell-O/pudding packets.

Proteins & Spreads:

Peanut butter or sunflower butter in plastic jars, beef jerky, bacon bits, shelf-stable sausage.

Drinks & Mixes:

Shelf-stable powdered milk, hot cocoa packets, powdered iced tea, Tang, Kool-Aid, lemonade mix, water enhancer packets.

Winter Warmth:

Hand warmers, thermal socks, fleece gloves, hats, lip balm, travel tissues.

☀️ Extreme Heat (85°F+ / Direct Sun)

Please skip the following items: Heat & Stop
Please consider donating these instead: Go & Stock
Meltable Treats & Sweets:

Chocolate, chocolate chips, gummies, marshmallows, suckers, hard candy, Little Debbie style pastry treats, and frosted bars.

Why: Enclosed outdoor pantry boxes trap heat like a parked car—meltables liquefy, stick to neighboring donations, and attract ants and wasps.
Heat-Safe Snacks:

Pretzels, Goldfish, crackers, trail mix, nuts, dried fruit, and fruit cups.

Warm-Weather Canned Goods & Liquids:

Pop-top soups, canned veggies, pasta sauce in plastic jars, applesauce pouches, and canned meals.

Warm-Weather Drinks:

Bottled water, sports drinks, juice boxes, and shelf-stable drink pouches.

Warm-Weather Extras:

Sunscreen packets, SPF lip balm, bug spray wipes, baseball cap, sun hat, bandana, and sunglasses.

Quick Weather Rules

When in doubt,
leave it out:

Factory-sealed, shelf-stable, and unexpired items only—skip anything questionable for the current forecast.

Plastic, pouches, and cans
over glass no matter what month:

Glass shatters in freezing temps and breaks easily if dropped on concrete year-round.

Following these guides can and will help Pantry Stewards.
Less Mess, Less Work, Less Wasted food.
