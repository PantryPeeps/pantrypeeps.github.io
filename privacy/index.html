Information collected through site analytics

Analytics may use cookies or similar technologies and process information such as pages viewed, interactions, referring pages, browser and device details, and approximate location derived from an internet connection. We do not ask you to create an account or enter payment details or other personal information on this website. You can manage or block cookies in your browser. You can also use Google's browser add-on to opt out of Google Analytics: Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.

Affiliate links are marked on the deal page, which also displays this disclosure: “As an Amazon Associate and Walmart Creator, I earn from qualifying purchases. #ad”. When you follow a retailer link, that retailer may collect information under its own privacy policy. We do not control retailer sites or their data practices.

This site is hosted through GitHub Pages. GitHub and Google may process technical information needed to host the site or provide analytics. Their own privacy policies describe how they handle that information.

Your choices and questions

You can limit cookies through your browser settings and choose whether to follow affiliate links. For privacy questions about Pantry Peeps, contact us through the Pantry Peeps channel where you found this site.

We may update this policy when the site or its services change. The updated date above will show when the policy was last revised.
