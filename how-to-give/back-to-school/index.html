Back-to-School Pantry Supplies

Towards the start of the school year, some Little Free Pantries accept basic school supplies or set out a dedicated drop-off bin beside the pantry. Every pantry has its own expectations for supply drop-offs and whether school items belong inside the main food cabinet, so please check each pantry’s posted steward rules first!

🎒 Suggested School Item Donations

  • Backpacks
  • Three-ring binders (all sizes)
  • Wide-ruled & college-ruled loose-leaf paper
  • Spiral notebooks
  • Primary composition books
  • #2 pencils & mechanical pencils
  • Blue & black pens
  • Highlighters
  • Erasers
  • 24-count crayons
  • Washable markers
  • Dry-erase markers
  • Blunt-tip kids’ safety scissors
  • Art smocks
  • Two-pocket folders
  • Subject dividers
  • Graph paper
  • Index cards
  • Sticky notes
  • Glue sticks
  • Zippered pencil pouches
  • Hard plastic pencil boxes
  • Basic wired earbuds
  • Scientific calculators
