Displaced Neighbor Care Kits & Holiday Ideas

Practical, respectful, and easy-to-carry kits for neighbors navigating house fires, evictions, sudden displacement, or living without a fixed address. Every item is chosen so it requires zero stove, zero can opener, and zero guesswork.

🎒 General Everyday Care Kit

  • Pop-top ready-to-eat meals (tuna/chicken salad kits, ravioli, Vienna sausages)
  • Bottled water, electrolyte packets & shelf-stable juice boxes
  • Soft granola bars, peanut butter crackers & applesauce cups
  • Fresh crew socks, body wipes, toothbrush/toothpaste & travel deodorant
  • Gallon resealable bag to keep items dry

🦃 Thanksgiving Kit Ideas

  • Pop-top turkey or chicken & gravy pouches
  • Instant mashed potato cups (just add hot water) & stuffing cups
  • Canned cranberry sauce with pull-tab lid & green beans
  • Shelf-stable pumpkin pie pudding cups or spiced applesauce
  • Warm socks & a handwritten holiday note

🎄 Christmas & Winter Holiday Ideas

  • Hot cocoa packets, tea bags & shelf-stable cookies or candy canes
  • Hearty pop-top soups, stews & crackers
  • Thermal socks, knit gloves, hand warmers & lip balm
  • Travel lotion, tissues & mini flashlight
  • Small puzzle book or deck of cards

🌼 Easter & Spring Ideas

  • Individually wrapped chocolates, jelly beans or pastel treats
  • Fruit cups, trail mix packs & spring crackers
  • Travel sunscreen, rain poncho & fresh ankle socks
  • Mini bubbles, stickers or small activity book for kids
